How clue rules work

Rules apply to your clue. Each group of A–Z letters counts as a word; punctuation splits words. Use 1–140 characters and at least one word. Do not name your exact answer as a whole word. Matching initials means any two clue words, not every word. Only your word and clue are sent to OpenAI.
