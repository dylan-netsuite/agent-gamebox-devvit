How to play

Tap a tile and type your own word. Write an across clue and a down clue, each with its own criterion. Match the crossing letters. Both clues must pass the AI review to open the next turn. You can play all five turns now.

Clues use 1–140 characters. Groups of A–Z letters count as words; punctuation separates them. Do not use your exact answer as a whole word in its clue. Only the word and clue are sent to OpenAI for review.
