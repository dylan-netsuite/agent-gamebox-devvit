An explorer’s guide

Write the crossword.
Find the world in it.

  1. Uncover two paths.

    Each turn reveals one Across and one Down word.

  2. Make the words yours.

    Tap a highlighted tile to type. Crossing letters connect your words; earlier accepted letters stay fixed.

  3. Leave two clues.

    Write a clue for each word and choose a separate rule for each. Both must pass the AI review to open the next turn.

Clues use 1–140 characters. Groups of A–Z letters count as words; punctuation separates them. Don’t include the exact answer as a whole word in its clue. Your word and clue are sent to OpenAI for review.
