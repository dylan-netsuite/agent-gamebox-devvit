How the rules are counted

Restrictions apply to the clue. Counting ignores case and treats each group of A–Z letters as a word; punctuation, hyphens, and apostrophes split words. Only the exact answer as a whole word is banned. Matching letters inside another word are allowed. There are no hidden substring or word-family bans.

All clues need at least one word and at most 140 characters. These seven restrictions are a proposed pool for this playtest.

Your word and clue go to an AI reviewer to check English word validity and clue fairness. Only the submitted text is sent to OpenAI; your Reddit identity stays on the game server.
