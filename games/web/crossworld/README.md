# CrossWorld · Sandy Shore & Haunted Hedge

Uncover a world, two crossword paths at a time. Each of five turns gives you one Across path and one Down path. Choose your own words, write their clues, and pick a separate clue criterion for each. Both worlds have ten paths and 33 tiles, with different layouts.

Open the map icon beside the CrossWorld wordmark to choose a world. **Sandy Shore** keeps its sand-and-sea-glass palette and shoreline discoveries. **Haunted Hedge** introduces lavender mist, plum controls, watchful hedges and crooked gates. Discovering paths transforms the garden; accepting both clues lights one of its five lanterns. Words do not need to match the world's theme.

Tap highlighted tiles to enter a word. Switch between **Across** and **Down** in the attached field notes. Tap the criterion symbol to choose from seven visual rules. Each clue keeps its own rule, and rules can be reused. Current shared letters update together; earlier accepted crossings stay fixed. Both clues must pass the AI review before the next turn opens. A partial pass leaves both words editable. On phones the map focuses on the active path; **Full map** shows the entire discovered world. Tap completed tiles to revisit their clues.

Drafts and completed pairs save to your Reddit account separately for each world. Switching waits for the current draft to save and stays put if that fails. Guests must sign in to save entered work before switching. Theme and motion preferences persist across world changes within the same browser session; sound is opt-in on each page.

Both worlds and all five turns are immediately available in this prototype. The **20-review daily allowance is shared between the two worlds**, and changing or restarting a world does not replenish it. Calendar gating, future-fill guarantees and sharing are not implemented. Existing Sandy Shore and earlier Chatterbloom records are preserved.

In the private playtest, **Settings → Start fresh** restarts only the selected world's board for your account. Review limits still apply, and old tabs must refresh before writing to a restarted board.

[Play the private Reddit test](https://www.reddit.com/r/crossworld_game_dev/?playtest=crossworld-game), choose **Explore CrossWorld**, then use the map icon to visit **Haunted Hedge**.
