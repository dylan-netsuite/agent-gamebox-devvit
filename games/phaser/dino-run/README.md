# Dino Run - T-Rex Runner

The classic Chrome dinosaur endless runner game, now on Reddit! Jump over cacti, duck under pterodactyls, and chase your high score.

## How It Works

A new Dino Run post appears in your subreddit. Tap the post to see your high score, then hit **PLAY** to jump into the full game.

## How to Play

1. **Jump** — Press Space, Up Arrow, or tap the screen to leap over obstacles
2. **Duck** — Press Down Arrow to crouch under flying pterodactyls (also fast-falls when airborne)
3. **Survive** — Dodge cacti and pterodactyls as the speed increases
4. **Score** — Points accumulate automatically as you run further
5. **Day/Night** — The world shifts between day and night every 700 points

## Features

- Endless runner with increasing difficulty
- Three obstacle types: small cacti, large cacti, cactus groups, and pterodactyls
- Day/night cycle that toggles the color palette
- Personal high score tracking
- Community leaderboard with top 10 scores
- Works on desktop and mobile
