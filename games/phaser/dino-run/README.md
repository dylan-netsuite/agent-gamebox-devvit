# Dino Run - T-Rex Runner

The classic Chrome dinosaur endless runner game, now on Reddit — and it's *hard*. Jump over cacti, duck under pterodactyls, and chase your high score as the world gets faster and faster.

## How It Works

A new Dino Run post appears in your subreddit. Tap the post to see your high score, then hit **PLAY** to jump into the full game.

## How to Play

1. **Jump** — Press Space, Up Arrow, or tap the screen to leap over obstacles
2. **Duck** — Press Down Arrow to crouch under flying pterodactyls (also fast-falls when airborne)
3. **Survive** — Dodge cacti and pterodactyls as the speed ramps up aggressively
4. **Score** — Points accumulate automatically as you run further
5. **Day/Night** — The world shifts between day and night every 500 points

## Features

- Brutally fast endless runner with steep difficulty curve
- Four obstacle types plus combo spawns (double obstacles at higher scores)
- Full particle effects: dust trails, landing impacts, speed lines, death explosions
- Day/night cycle with moon, twinkling stars, and parallax mountains
- Score milestone flashes every 100 points
- Personal high score tracking and community leaderboard
- Works on desktop and mobile
